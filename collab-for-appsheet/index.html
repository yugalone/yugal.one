Core Features

Auto-sync
Keep your data up to date by turning on auto-sync and setting a sync interval that suits your operational needs.
Sleek typography
Elevate your interface with a simple switch that applies Collab Sans or Inter fonts across the system, delivering a clean and minimalist visual experience.
Seamless printing
Effortlessly print your table and deck views that preserve all filters, groupings, and their corresponding aggregates, while seamlessly embedding your branding, title, and metadata.
100% local & secure
Process data directly within your browser sandbox, ensuring your business data is never transmitted to any external servers or third-party APIs.
