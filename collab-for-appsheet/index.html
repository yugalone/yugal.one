Core Capabilities
Auto-sync
Keep your data up to date by turning on automated background sync and setting a refresh interval that suits your operational needs.
Sleek Typography
Elevate your interface with a simple switch that applies Collab Sans or Inter fonts across the system, delivering a clean and minimalist visual experience.
Seamless Printing
Effortlessly print table and deck views that preserve active filters, groupings, and aggregates, while embedding your branding, title, and metadata.
100% Local & Secure
All DOM processing runs locally within your browser sandbox. Your data is never transmitted to any external servers or third-party APIs.
