## 2. Description of the Service

Flows is a document and label mail-merge web application designed by **Yugal** that runs inside Google Apps Script and your web browser. Flows enables you to:
* Connect data from Google Spreadsheets, Google Forms, and Drive CSV files.
* Design document and label layouts on a scaled visual print canvas.
* Insert dynamic data chips, barcodes, QR codes, tables, and system tokens.
* Preview merged records interactively.
* Export documents as single or individual PDF and Markdown files, save directly to Google Drive, or print straight from the browser.
