Collab

Experience across finance, accounting, and auditing surfaced a recurring pattern: teams need dependable workflow automation, but traditional enterprise software is either too rigid or too expensive to customize.

Organizations run on interconnected processes, but most enterprise tools force teams into fragmented silos. By deconstructing the functional architecture of major enterprise applications, Collab rebuilds foundational operational patterns for no-code environments, pairing AppSheet with structured relational modeling, targeted scripting, and robust automation logic.

Collab unifies contacts, multi-tier bill of materials, inventory tracking, manufacturing runs, chart of accounts, and financial reporting under a single, cohesive multi-tenant architecture. Built with standardized design patterns and reusable modularity, it lets organizations deploy quickly, scale cleanly, and maintain operational integrity without traditional software development overhead.
