Enterprise Operations Platform

Collab

Experience across finance, accounting, and auditing surfaced a recurring pattern: teams needed dependable workflow automation, but tools were either too rigid or too complex to implement. That gap led to building Collab—an application at the intersection of finance, operations, and design that turns real business logic into scalable, no-code workflows.

The problem is fundamental: organizations run on interconnected workflows, but most tools force teams into fragmented systems. Instead of juggling separate applications for contacts, inventory, production, accounting, and documentation, Collab unifies everything in one place—where data, processes, and teams operate seamlessly, and each workflow flows naturally into the next.

The result: enterprise-grade structure without traditional development overhead. Powered by relational modeling, automated workflows, and standardized design patterns, Collab lets organizations deploy quickly, scale cleanly, and maintain operational integrity—staying practical as they grow, without sacrificing the flexibility to evolve.

Capabilities

Core Operations Architecture

Contacts Directory
Centralize your internal and external stakeholders into a single, authoritative source of truth, equipped to link multiple bank accounts and dedicated assets directly to each profile.
Inventory Management
Track item availability, product lifecycles, and dynamic pricing through advanced categorization and grouping.
Bill of Materials (BOM)
List required raw materials and quantities, alongside process-absorbed consumables, using a predefined cost structure.
Movement Tracking
Monitor and capture inventory transit, stock adjustments, and transfers on the fly.
Manufacturing
Orchestrate complex, multi-tiered production workflows that are intricately linked to live inventory data.
Chart of Accounts
Record financial transactions within a defined hierarchy to achieve granular visibility across all fiscal activities.
Financial Reporting
Bridge financial records with inventory movements so your financials reflect true operational reality.
Document Management
Store your documents in a centralized repository to streamline context, collaboration, and document flow.
Ticketing
Manage, track, and resolve internal and external requests, tasks, or issues efficiently to ensure timely support.
