Collab

Experience across finance, accounting, and auditing surfaced a recurring pattern: teams need dependable workflow automation, but traditional software is either too rigid or too expensive to customize.

Organizations run on interconnected processes, but most enterprise tools force teams into fragmented silos. Collab unifies contacts, multi-tier bill of materials, inventory tracking, manufacturing runs, chart of accounts, and financial reporting into a single, cohesive multi-tenant AppSheet architecture.

Powered by relational modeling, automated workflows, and standardized design patterns, Collab lets organizations deploy quickly, scale cleanly, and maintain operational integrity without traditional software development overhead.
